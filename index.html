Capabilities · SRS §2–3

Everything a physical corkboard wishes it could do

◈

Mixed media nodes

Text, images, video, links, files and audio live side by side on one board.

⟡

Thought connections

Draw labelled relationships — Supports, Causes, Depends on — between any two nodes.

⬔

Groups & tags

Cluster related ideas into groups, tag anything, then filter the board in one click.

◎

Infinite canvas

Pan and zoom across a generous workspace with buttery drag physics.

⬣

Share with control

Private by default. Invite collaborators as viewers or editors, or publish a link.

✎

Comment anywhere

Discuss the whole board or annotate a single node without leaving context.
